# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, harassment-free community for contributors and users.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Focus feedback on code and outcomes, not individuals.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Abusive or threatening language.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or moderate contributions that violate this code.
